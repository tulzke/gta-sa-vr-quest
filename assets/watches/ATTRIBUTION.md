Modified for GTA San Andreas VR Quest. The project owner simplified the watch
geometry and dial and fitted the model to the reference hand.
The project export excludes reference hands, gloves and other scene objects,
preserves the watch's fit in the reference hand's coordinate frame, prepares
planar UVs for the interface surface, and converts the watch to glTF 2.0 GLB.
